# PVC laser stand interfaces

Issue: [#46](https://github.com/ShakataGaNai/b123d/issues/46). Dimensions in mm.

User measurement (2026-10-28): laser housing is round, diameter 18 mm. Model,
revision and usable straight barrel length are unknown. The [reference image](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/51bMQ6MBcIL._AC_SL1500_.jpg)
was inspected; it depicts a removable clamp and wired cylindrical laser. Fit is
to the bare barrel with the original clamp removed; no dimensions inferred from pixels.
Downloaded reference is in ignored outputs/pvc_laser_stand/reference.jpg;
redistribution permission unknown, image not committed.

[Charlotte Pipe Schedule 40 table](https://www.charlottepipe.com/uploads/documents/technical/BR-PK.pdf)
lists 3/4 inch PVC OD 1.050 inches, ID 0.824 inches, wall 0.113 inches:
26.670, 20.9296 and 2.8702 mm respectively. These are nominal dimensions,
not measurements of the user's pipe. Pipe ID/ovality and cut squareness vary.

Design allowances, not source facts: 20.90 mm insert envelope, 4 mm cross webs,
1 mm entry taper, 18.30 mm bore, 24 mm sleeve length, 3.4 mm plain pilots for
assumed M4 coarse screws to form threads once in PETG. No standard thread is
modeled or certified. Try screw engagement and pipe fit before printing all parts.
Ground stake length 120 mm is a design assumption for ordinary soft soil.
