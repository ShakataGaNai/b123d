# Community-published Multiboard dimensions

Research accessed 2026-10-01. Four Terra research workers searched original discussions, creator-owned parametric CAD, rail interfaces, and thread/drawing guides. This record extends the [adapter reference](README.md); it is not an official interface standard. No downloaded CAD code was executed, no new part was built, and no physical fit was tested.

## Answer to the specification question

Other creators do publish useful dimensions. The strongest independently checked near-match to the author's [13.5 mm across-flats AirTag peg](measured-connectors.md) is **k0nze's 13.45 mm across-flats male octagonal peg**, with an 11-coordinate-unit axial length. There is also a creator-owned dimensioned tile drawing and a source-pinned parametric tile reconstruction containing hole, thickness, thread and grid-phase values.

The author confirms that the octagonal pegs insert into **snaps** and selects **13.5 mm across flats × 6.5 mm projection** for the reference interface. The 13.5 mm dimension is width, not depth. k0nze's 11-unit length describes a different adapter and is not our target. The exact snap variant/revision remains unspecified; the receiver family is no longer an open question. The snap itself engages the tile's large opening.

Values below are native CAD model coordinates, conventionally interpreted as millimeters for these print designs; where the source does not explicitly declare units, that interpretation is an assumption. Across-flats values were checked from the construction, not inferred from variable names alone.

The 0.05-unit difference between the author's male AirTag peg and k0nze's male peg is **not a measured socket clearance**. Their axial lengths also differ. Preserve both designs' parameters rather than silently averaging them or adopting a universal tolerance.

## Evidence boundaries and verification

- Official [Core Parts Documentation §§2.3–3](https://docs.multibuild.io/beginner-section/core-parts-documentation#23-mid-hole) identifies friction-fit inserts as mating to a snap's Mid Hole; large board holes instead take the snaps themselves or large threads.
- Parent independently read the pinned k0nze module **and Boolean caller**, the pinned Pip! Gold parameters and hole/placement construction, the pinned IOIO72 rail-slot caller, and trenaryja's SVG annotations plus author statement preserved by [PullPush](https://api.pullpush.io/reddit/submission/search?ids=1h7wsmg). PullPush is a third-party archive of original text, not verification of the current live Reddit post or archive completeness.
- Parent opened the [BambuLab specification discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/BambuLab/comments/1rxqz0a/multiboard_connection_specs/) in Chromium and read the live author's request. It describes an octagonal solid entering a snap's center and asks for published cleat-slot sizes; it is not a completed dimension specification. Direct reader/JSON routes were blocked, and RSS was rate-limited. No login was performed.
- Search-engine synthesized numbers are leads only. Unverified radius/across-flats interpretations, arbitrary clearance recommendations and name-based variant matches were not accepted as interface facts.

Community geometry improves the design starting point but does not establish physical fit, production-revision compatibility, installed rail clearance or redistribution rights. The user closed [issue #10](https://github.com/ShakataGaNai/b123d/issues/10): use the known peg geometry and revisit compatibility only if print testing exposes a problem.
