- **Revision scope:** live official ecosystem and documentation as accessed on the research date; full license marked **19 December 2025**; Community Promise effective **12 December 2025**. Individual CAD/STL revisions are **not identified by this overview**. A subsystem name or library filter is not a mechanical revision.

Missing dimensions, test conditions, and unresolved legal wording remain unknown.
- **Evidence classes:** **authoritative** below means the publisher states it, not that it has been independently proven; **measured** would require an identified specimen/method (none here); **derived** means explicit calculation from cited values; **assumed** would mean an unsupported choice (no mating allowances are adopted). Recommendations identified as **[INFERENCE]** are engineering/release decisions, not vendor specifications.

## Naming and ecosystem map

### What changed, and what did not

The official story attributes creation to Jonathan Kobylanski / Keep Making and describes release in **December 2023** after **five months** of development/testing. That is a retrospective product-history statement, not a revision date for today's files. The old site used **Multiboard** as the all-in-one umbrella; the official new-site announcement explicitly says the umbrella is now **MultiBuild** because it includes more than a board. **MultiBoard remains the board subsystem**, with **MultiBin** alongside it and additional subsystems planned. The exact rebrand announcement date is unknown; do not backdate the MultiBuild name to claim a particular file was already released under that name. [E1, E9, H1]

| Current concept | Relationship / relevant custom-holder route | Name/revision cautions and primary evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **MultiBuild** | Umbrella ecosystem for interoperating boards, bins, drawers, mounts and structural accessories | Not synonymous with one tile or one mating profile. [E1–E3, E9] |
| **MultiBoard** | Connection boards/plates, mounting systems, snaps, threads, hooks, shelves, brackets, beams and related accessories | Library now uses “Plates” in categories while beginner docs still say “Tiles.” Do not infer all plate types have the same holes, thickness, or edge features. [E4, E6] |
| **MultiBin** | Shells on plates/grids, inserts, tops, dividers, labels/lids, drawer systems; bins can attach to boards or to other bins | A CU describes modular sizing, not the clear interior or external envelope of every shell. Inserts/tops must match the selected shell revision and features. [E3, E4, E6] |
| **Fix-Points / Rails** | Sliding connector family bridging MultiBin accessories and MultiBoard; rails also connect shells | Core docs explicitly state **“Multipoints” renamed to “Fix-Points”; “Multipoint Rails” now “Rails.”** Old headings and learning-pack names remain. They are not an additional currently separate subsystem in the current library. [E4, E6 §Multipoint] |
| **Cross-Compatibility** | Access route for adapters to other storage systems | A category, not a guarantee that any HSW/pegboard/Gridfinity-shaped object fits any MultiBuild part. Legacy site advertises HSW push-fits and pegboard accessories; exact adapter/third-party interface needs its own reference. [E4, H1] |
| **Legacy Parts** | Search older/superseded variants when identifying an existing installation | Promise only commits to reasonable efforts for legacy access; removal of obsolete parts is allowed. Do not assume legacy and current mating features are interchangeable. [E4, L4 Core Commitments] |
| **Remixing Files** | Official route for interface references useful to custom holder design | Legacy homepage advertises STEP and STL core-component remixing files; file-format access is not an open-source or redistribution grant. Exact current downloadable inventory not verified here. [E4, H1, L2] |
| **Tools / generators** | Official planner and geometry/customization tools | A generator result is still governed by applicable terms; downloadable generator source, native author CAD, and a stable public parameter API are not established here. [E7, L4 Definitions] |

### Interface-choice map for a holder

All functional relationships in this table are **authoritative descriptions** from [E6](https://docs.multibuild.io/beginner-section/core-parts-documentation), not measurements of mating geometry.

| Family | Installation / retention | Consequence for future holder design |
|---|---|---|
| **Small tile holes** | Take Peg Click accessories, Small Threads, and non-printed pegboard accessories | Peg Click connects to two small holes; exact centers, insertion shape and rear keep-out still require the selected reference. |
| **Large tile holes / Multiholes** | Take Snaps or Large Threads | Choose direct threaded attachment or a snap intermediary; the grid spacing alone cannot generate the latch or thread. |
| **Regular / moderate / heavy snaps** | Regular symmetrical; moderate and heavy carry load in one direction and enter at an angle; heavy requires wall-offset tiles | Record insertion direction and loading direction; reserve rear space for the exact snap. “Heavy” is a family label, not a universal numeric rating. |
| **Double-sided snaps** | Parts A/B capture opposite tile sides; used for removable surface-mounted or double-sided/freestanding tiles | Keep both faces and assembly order accessible. Offset Snap Mount Part A is described as creating a surface offset and containing a screw hole. |
| **Snap Mid Hole** | Takes Bolt-Locked Inserts, Friction-Fit Inserts or Mid Threads | A bolt-locked holder needs locking-bolt/tool access; a friction-fit holder remains pull-out removable and is described for lightweight accessories. |
| **Printed threads** | Small into small tile holes; Mid into snap mid holes; Large into multiholes | Thread family names are not ISO metric screw designations. Publisher suggests attaching remixed accessories using a hole and threads; bore/head/washer dimensions still need evidence. |
| **Fix-Point / old Multipoint** | Accessory slides on/off a matching negative hole/rail; board connection may use Small/Mid/Large Threads or Bolt-Locking | Preserve slide travel, insertion direction and stop/locking features rather than only a static bounding box. |
| **Regular vs Lite Multipoint** | Regular mates a Multipoint Hole; Lite mates negative Multipoint Rails | E6 says Lite is **1 mm thinner** than Regular (relative thickness difference, no absolute thickness/datum/tolerance supplied). This is not enough to reconstruct either profile. |
| **Shell rails** | Fix-Points for bin-to-tile; Rail Click Connector for bin-to-bin; Rail Pop-In for bin-to-bin or bin-to-tile | Rail Click slides/clicks in; Pop-In unfolds/pops in and does not slide on/off. They have different assembly motion and retention. |
| **MultiBin plates / base plates** | Shells sit on plates; Dual Clips connect plates; vertical plates use Bar Clips; base plates add mounting connection holes | Vertical restraint and supported bin orientation must be explicit; a loose gravity-seated bin is not automatically wall-secure. |
| **Shell inserts/tops/drawers** | Inserts add internal function; tops use Top Click Rims; simple drawers use Stopper Pins; shells attach to tiles through Fix-Points | Distinguish bin-interior insert, lid/rim connection, and structural board connection. Drawer withdrawal changes the loaded envelope. |

### Units and datums: library names are not dimensions of mating features

| Source convention | Stated value | Evidence class / locator | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| MU, **Multi Unit** | Based on **25 mm** sizing | Authoritative, E6 Measurement System | Modular linear sizing/grid basis; not a bore diameter or connector clearance |
| CU, **Cell Unit** | Based on **50 mm** sizing | Authoritative, E6 Measurement System and Shell Dimensions | Shell modular sizing; E6 states another shell of 1 CU height increases stack height by 50 mm |
| In-plane conversion | **2 × 2 MU = 1 × 1 CU** | Authoritative, E6 Measurement System | In-plane footprint conversion; each CU grid interval spans two MU intervals. Not a volume equation or a complete part envelope |
| Dimension order | **Width (front) × Depth (side) × Height** | Authoritative, E6 Measurement System | Store axis labels and units with every library choice; do not blindly map these onto STL XYZ |
| Recommended mounting tile size | **8 × 8 MU; 200 × 200 mm** | Authoritative, P2c/P2d Get The Parts | Stated recommended planar size; no thickness or measured manufacturing tolerance established by these illustrations |

This overview adopts **mm for lengths** and **degrees for angles**, but adopts **no global CAD origin or source-to-build123d transform**: neither library front/side naming nor printer Z defines a universal board datum. Future records must identify the board front, mating plane, grid origin, positive axes, gravity/loading direction, installation orientation, and imported-file transform. Printer orientation and installed orientation are different contracts. [E6, P1; **[INFERENCE]** workflow]

## Printing and fit: documented defaults, not universal settings

### Default manufacturing recommendations

All rows below are **authoritative publisher recommendations** in [P1](https://docs.multibuild.io/beginner-section/printing-guidelines), with no independent printed-specimen measurement. The page explicitly says **some parts have different settings; read each part's description**.

| Item | Official recommendation / convention | Boundary for custom holders |
|---|---|---|
| Perimeters | **3 perimeter walls** (the page says “3 perimeter wall thickness”) | Count of walls, **not 3 mm** wall thickness; actual thickness depends on extrusion width |
| Layer height | **0.2 mm** vertical layer increment | Keep print layer height separate from feature clearance |
| Infill | **15%** | Pattern, top/bottom layers and custom holder stress requirements not specified |
| Supports | **No supports** | Applies to supplied parts/orientation, not every newly designed holder |
| Nozzle | **0.4 mm diameter** nominal nozzle size | Not a guarantee of dimensional accuracy |
| Orientation / file | Use orientation supplied in the **STL** files; otherwise printer defaults | Reorienting alters the documented process; STL itself does not carry a guaranteed unit standard, so confirm interpreted mm against a known dimension |
| Material | **Matte PLA** | P1 additionally claims matte PLA is stronger than regular PLA, but provides no formulation, test data or universal comparison; preserve as a vendor claim, not a material law |
| Most-part tolerance | **0.25 mm** | P1 does not specify per-face, radial, diametral, total gap, or manufacturing uncertainty; it is **not** permission to add 0.25 mm to every mating dimension |
| Overhang design guidance | As close to **45°** as possible; sometimes **50° or 60°** | Angle reference and exact affected faces not defined on the page; this is not a dimensional interface tolerance |

For a future holder, **[INFERENCE]** separate nominal mating geometry from intentional fit clearance, printer compensation, and structural material allowance. Print a reference and a small representative mating coupon on the actual printer/material/settings, assess assembly/removal/locking, and record any compensation with its direction and clearance convention. Do not scale an entire board interface to solve one tight feature without checking the grid spacing and threads again. This is a proposed verification method, not a measured fit result.

### Calibration references and limits

The first-party **Crystal Clearance Tower & Tolerance Card**, [model 362850](https://thangs.com/designer/MultiBuild/3d-model/Crystal%20Clearance%20Tower%20%26%20Tolerance%20Card-362850), describes itself only as a quick clearance/tolerance test. Public discussion includes interpretations of ring travel and dimensional numbers, but those comments are **community assertions**, not an authoritative mapping from a test result to a particular snap clearance. The author's accessible description did not establish the card's full measurement method or universal pass/fail threshold. Do not promote commenters' advice to vendor instructions. [P3]

Use the official [Test Files category](https://multibuild.io/parts/test-files) as an access route; current category inventory was not enumerated in this session. Some library/generator parts have tolerance choices, and the Bolt Generator advertises adjustment, but a named “Tight”/“Loose” variant must be tied to **that part's exact file/revision** and its own stated convention. No universal alternate numerical tolerance is adopted here. [E4, E7]

### Stack printing and shrinkage

| Method / condition | Official details and units | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| **Ironing stacks** | Individual prints separated by **0.2 mm vertical gap**; turn on ironing for top surfaces; optional random seams described as stronger; same default 3 walls, 0.2 mm layers, 15% infill, no supports. Test: [model 977033](https://than.gs/m/977033). [P1] | One tile face is not as good as when printed individually; relevant to double-sided use. Separation gap is not connector clearance |
| **Multi-material stacks** | Advanced PLA/PETG method; split STL by objects, assign tiles and spacers opposite materials, match PETG plate temperature to PLA; PETG flow ratio/extrusion multiplier **1–1.3**, requiring tests. Author's example **1.1** on Bambu Lab. Test: [model 1108818](https://than.gs/m/1108818). [P1] | Ratios are dimensionless process settings, not scale factors. Publisher does not recommend manual pauses without a multi-material printer; method may be slower but improves separation/bottom finish |
| **Tall-bin shrinkage** | P1 flags bins taller than **2 cells / 100 mm**; recommends Z-axis compensation and reports personal PLA bin scale of **100.5%**. [P1] | Material/brand/color/humidity/temperature dependence explicitly acknowledged; no universal correction. Scale applies to printer Z in the selected orientation, not automatically the installed bin height |

The 100.5% example is **derived** as a factor **1.005** applied to nominal slicer-Z dimension, an increase of **0.5%** (`1.005 − 1`); it is the author's example, not a measured value for this project's material. Global Z scaling can change mating feature positions as well as total height. **[INFERENCE]** verify rails, drawer travel and rims after any such correction. There is no evidence here for a standard nozzle/bed temperature, PETG/ABS/ASA substitute process, creep allowance, outdoor exposure limit, or safety factor for custom parts. [P1]

## Strength and installation: reported figures must stay attached to their evidence

**No universal safe working load was established.** The sources below report kilograms as supported mass, not force in newtons. This record does not convert them into an engineering allowable, multiply them by connector count, or assign the maximum to every attachment.

| Source claim | Evidence class / exact scope | Missing test conditions / usable conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Current page: “Hold a LOT of weight (**Up to 46 kg**)” | Authoritative marketing statement, E2 “Attach your stuff” | No exact part/file revision, number of attachment points, lever arm, loading direction, duration, temperature, material/profile, wall anchorage, failure criterion, repeat count or safety factor supplied. **Not a per-hole, per-snap, per-holder, or installation rating** |
| Legacy: each peg hole and T-bolt can hold about **20 kg** “in our testing”; “make sure to test yourself” | Authoritative legacy publisher-reported test result, H1 “Incredible Strength”; no independent measurement | Exact test article/revision and protocol absent. Applies to the named peg-hole/T-bolt claim only, not every connection |
| Same legacy FAQ: “up to **18 kg**” | Authoritative legacy marketing/FAQ statement, H1 | Different scope/conditions are not established. Do not average with 20 kg or assume a known upgrade explains 46 kg |
| Moderate/heavy weight-bearing snaps work in **one direction** and require angled insertion; heavy only with wall offset | Authoritative functional restriction, E6 §2.1 | Directional retention matters even without a number. Reversing installed orientation or pulling away from the board is not proven by a downward load description |
| Friction-fit inserts for lightweight hooks/pegs; bolt-lock prevents pulling the insert out of the snap | Authoritative functional description, E6 §3 | This describes retention, not the ultimate strength of the holder, snap, bolt or wall mounting |

The full license expressly makes weight limits/material/production suggestions general rather than professional advice, gives no suitability warranty, and places material/production and installation care on the user. [L2 §2.2 and §2.4]

**[INFERENCE] engineering consequence:** treat a loaded object, holder body, insert/thread/Fix-Point, snap/tile, mounting pillar, screw/anchor, backing and wall as one load path. Record object mass, center-of-mass stand-off and handling forces; a longer holder changes bending/pull-out demand even at the same mass. Wall-fastener suitability, installation workmanship, printed-layer direction, temperature, creep over time and dynamic insertion/removal remain part of validation. No tested custom-holder or wall rating is supplied here; avoid overhead/safety-critical use on the basis of marketing maxima alone.

### Membership is access, not ownership

As publicly rendered on the access date, [A1](https://multibuild.io/memberships) advertises free No Login library/hub/individual-part access and free-account dashboard/community/feedback; **Multi Tinkerer USD 5/month** for learning/simple/mounting/stack packs and **3 months** early access; **Multi Maker USD 10/month** adds advanced packs, generators and expert help; [A2](https://multibuild.io/multi-partner) advertises **Multi Partner USD 42/month** including previous-tier perks and physical-print commercial licensing. These are displayed monthly prices, not a tax-inclusive quote or a promise of future price. L2, not tier marketing, supplies the commercial rights/cap. Thangs Link connects existing membership to MultiBuild. [A1, A2, E9]

The Promise says functional Core/Advanced individual parts must become free for Personal Use within **six months** after first official Release, and free parts or functional successors remain free while active. **Packs, Generated Parts, Cosmetic Parts and Branded Parts are exceptions**, so “all parts free” marketing cannot be read as every download and custom generator output being free immediately. Existing free core versions are protected from ordinary paid reclassification; legacy/obsolete removal is allowed. [L4 Core Commitments and Definitions]

| **Personal business use** | L4 includes internal/client-facing business use if no physical parts sold/leased/rented, while L2 §2.1.5 income wording is broad | L1 says Promise prevails, but this record does not supply a legal determination for paid installations/services |

## Short designer release checklist

Before releasing a future custom holder/adapter:

- Identify exact board/bin/connector variants and revisions, front/mating datums, units, loading direction and insertion/removal travel; do not turn MU/CU into a guessed mating profile.
- Record original reference URLs, file names/revisions, access dates and permission basis; keep vendor original files out of public source/assets unless separately permitted. Hash original bytes only when permitted intake actually occurs.
- Separate original holder geometry from copied vendor geometry/code; assess substantial-change and mixed-license obligations under the **full** terms, not “free download” labels. Obtain clarification for unresolved definitions or proposed paid digital distribution.
- Provide MultiBoard/MultiBuild attribution, original-part and license links, clear modification notes and no implied endorsement; preserve embedded notices and third-party permissions.
- Publish nominal geometry and chosen print compensation as different values with declared clearance conventions; test representative reference/coupon and complete printed assembly on the stated material/settings/orientation. Do not present the general 0.25 mm recommendation as a universal radial gap.
- State tested object/holder/connector/mount/wall configuration, load direction/lever arm, duration and result. No universal load rating; unchanged official parts do not prove a new holder or wall installation safe.
- For physical sales, verify active Multi Partner entitlement, third-party remix consent, cap calculation and proposed manufacturing process. Recheck current license/Promise/membership terms before release.

These are **[INFERENCE]** release controls based on the identified unknowns and retrieved obligations; no physical fit/strength test was performed for this record.
